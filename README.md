## 🎯 Project Objective
- Compare sales performance across 
  any two regions simultaneously
- Classify sub-category performance 
  using profit ratio KPIs
- Forecast quarterly profit trends 
  with confidence intervals
- Detect outliers and segment customers 
  through advanced analytics

---

## 📊 What's Inside — Story Panels

### Panel 1: Dual Region Dashboard
- Side-by-side region comparison
- Dynamic Parameters for Measures 
  (Sales, Profit, Quantity) and 
  Dimensions (Sub-Category, Category)
- KPI tables with 5 metrics per region
- Sparklines with Min/Max Sales tracking
- First Order Date per region

### Panel 2: KPI Classification Chart
- ProfitR% Calculated Field
- Conditional shape encoding:
  🟢 High Performance (> +10%)
  🔴 Low Performance (< -10%)
  🟡 Neutral (-10% to +10%)
- Quarter-level filter with slider
- 17 sub-categories × 4 regions

### Panel 3: Profit Forecasting
- Quarterly actual profits (2021-2024)
- Projected estimates (2025-2026)
- Confidence interval bands
- Peak profit: ₹38,195 in Q3 2023

### Panel 4: Clustering Analysis
- Customer/product segmentation
- Pattern identification across regions

### Panel 5: Box Plot Analysis
- Sales distribution by Category
- Profit encoded as color gradient
- Outlier identification
- Technology shows widest distribution

### Panel 6: Sales Trend By Year
- Year-over-year comparison (2021-2024)
- 2024 consistently outperforms 
  all previous years from Q3 onwards

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- **West region** generates highest 
  total sales (₹739,814) but South 
  has higher Avg Sales per transaction
- **Copiers and Phones** are top 
  profit drivers in West region
- **Tables** consistently show 
  negative profit across multiple 
  regions — pricing strategy needed
- **2024 Q3** shows strongest growth 
  momentum across all regions
- **Technology category** has highest 
  sales outliers — indicating 
  premium product transactions
